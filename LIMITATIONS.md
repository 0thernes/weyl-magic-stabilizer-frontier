# Limitations, Boundaries, and Real-vs-Metaphor Audit (`LIMITATIONS.md`)

To uphold absolute epistemic clarity (Playbook v1.3 & Build Standard B41), this document records every explicit boundary, non-claim, and scope constraint of `weyl-magic-stabilizer-frontier` (`0TH-2026-021`, `v2.0.0`).

## 1. Explicit Non-Claims (What We Do NOT Prove)
1. **Mega-Atlas Grand Problems Retired: `0 / 2,000`.** None of the 2,000 overarching Mega-Atlas grand open problems are retired by this work (see [`docs/MEGA_ATLAS_CROSSCHECK.md`](docs/MEGA_ATLAS_CROSSCHECK.md)).
2. **Low-Mana Regime of Erew–Goldstein Conjecture 2 (`arXiv:2512.19657v2`):** Theorem `T2b` (`trace_norm_spectral_lock`) proves Conjecture 2 across the entire high-mana regime $\mathcal{M}(\ket{\psi}) > \frac{1}{2}\ln(D/2)$. When $\mathcal{M}(\ket{\psi}) \le \frac{1}{2}\ln(D/2)$, the Frobenius trace identity $\mathrm{Tr}(H_s^2)=D$ alone is mathematically insufficient to enforce a spectral gap (formally proved in `AdversarialTests.refute_at03_spectral_lock_without_high_mana`). Although our reproducible sweep (`results/numerical_claims_audit.json`) finds zero counterexamples across `15,566` single-qudit (`13,215` nowhere-vanishing) and `2,678` two-qutrit (`1,344` nowhere-vanishing) 1D Clifford eigenstates and verifies local maximality of an explicit low-mana 3-qutrit witness ($D=27$), low-mana Conjecture 2 remains open.
3. **Global Upper Bound for Two-Qudit Prime $M_2$ (`arXiv:2607.07197` Eq. 59):** Theorem `T3a` proves universal Alltop attainment $M_2^{\max}(\mathbb{C}^d\otimes\mathbb{C}^d) \ge \ln[d^4/(2d^2-1)]$ for every prime $d\ge 5$, and `T3c` proves both primality of $d$ and $n=2$ are necessary. Proving the matching global upper bound $M_2(\ket{\psi}) \le \ln[d^4/(2d^2-1)]$ for all pure states $\ket{\psi}\in\mathbb{C}^d\otimes\mathbb{C}^d$ ($d\ge 5$ prime) remains open.
4. **Zauner's SIC-POVM Existence Conjecture:** Theorem `T1c` proves that any order-3 Zauner Clifford symmetry with $|\mathrm{Tr}(Z)|^2\le 3$ forces eigenspace dimension $m_\lambda\ge 2$ ($\forall d\ge 4$ canonical; $\forall d\ge 7$ all 3). We do not prove the existence or non-existence of WH-SIC fiducials in arbitrary dimension $d$.
5. **Kulhandjian–Hanzo Conjecture 27 Lower Bound for $p\ge 11$ (`arXiv:2610.02392`):** Theorem `T4b` proves the universal upper bound $d_{\min}(\mathcal{Q}(p,2))\le p+1$ for all odd primes $p\ge 3$, and `C4d` settles $d_{\min}(\mathcal{Q}(5,2))=6$ and $d_{\min}(\mathcal{Q}(7,2))=8$ exhaustively. Establishing the matching lower bound $d_{\min}(\mathcal{Q}(p,2))\ge p+1$ for $p\ge 11$ ($\binom{133}{11}$ subsets) requires an algebraic rather than orbit-enumeration proof and remains open.
6. **Exact Exhaustive Lower Bound `C4d` Is `[EXACT-COMP]`, Not Lean-Kernel:** The upper bound $d_{\min}(\mathcal{Q}(7,2))\le 8$ (`T4b`, `T4c`) is `[LEAN-6K]` kernel-checked. The matching exhaustive lower bound $d_{\min}(\mathcal{Q}(7,2))\ge 8$ over $1{,}546{,}218$ orbits (`C4d`) is independently certified in zero-unsafe Rust and strict ISO C11 + x86-64 NASM assembly (`[EXACT-COMP]`), agreeing with Theorem 28 of `arXiv:2610.02392`; replaying $1.54\times 10^6$ $\mathbb{F}_7$ Gaussian eliminations inside the Lean reduction kernel was deliberately kept out of the kernel trust base to preserve fast 6-kernel CI replay.

## 2. Real-vs-Metaphor & Software Engineering Boundaries
- **Deterministic Software QRNG Mixer (`quantum_stack/qrng_mixer.c`):** Despite the "QRNG" project-stack name, this binary implements a strictly deterministic, cryptographically auditable classical PRNG (SplitMix64 + xoshiro256**, default seed `20261126ULL` XORed with `0x5745594C4D414749ULL`). It uses **zero quantum hardware entropy** and is explicitly labeled as a deterministic software Born-rule sampler (`4,096` shots per Pauli word).
- **Moonlab WASM, QGTL C & Numerical Sweeps (`[NUM-STACK]`):** IEEE-754 double-precision floating-point arithmetic in `quantum_stack/moonlab_super_mub.mjs`, `quantum_stack/qgtl_sic_metric.c`, and `scripts/verify_numerical_claims.py` provides independent physical/geometric confirmation to $\sim 10^{-14}$–$10^{-16}$ tolerance, never formal proof.
- **Eshkol v1.3.6 Availability in CI:** Because Eshkol v1.3.6 is a custom local LLVM toolchain (`Z:\Tools\eshkol`), `eshkol/weyl_magic_ad.esk` and its captured execution log `results/eshkol_audit.log` are archived and verified locally; GitHub Actions runs the other eight portable toolchains (Lean 4.35.0 6-kernel, Rust, Zig 0.17, ISO C11 + NASM, ISO C++20, Moonlab WASM, QGTL C, Python) on cloud runners.
